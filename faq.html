Frequently Asked Questions

What is “What’s in the Water”?

A community-driven water quality monitoring program based in Fort Myers Beach, where volunteers help collect samples across the Estero Bay watershed.

Do I need experience to volunteer?

No experience is required! Volunteers of all backgrounds are welcome and guided through the sampling process.

How long does sampling take?

Sampling typically takes about 1–2 hours, including pick-up and drop-off.

What do volunteers actually do?

Volunteers collect water samples, record observations, and help support community-driven environmental monitoring.

Do I need to bring anything?

No, sample kits are pre-made and will be ready for you to pick up at The Mound House.
